## 0. Hard rules (never break)

1. **Honesty.** No testimonials, client names/logos, case studies, real-looking stats, team members, headcounts, locations, founding dates, awards, or compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO, HIPAA...). Anything illustrative is labelled as an example on the element itself.
2. **No AI attribution anywhere in the codebase**, commits included (no "generated by", no assistant names, no CLAUDE.md, no AI comments).
3. **Secrets never committed.** The Discord webhook URL lives only in `.env.local` (gitignored) and in Vercel env vars as `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL`. Never print it in docs, code, logs or client bundles.
4. **Zero em dashes or en dashes in visible copy.** Use hyphens, commas, periods, colons.
5. **Single dark theme.** No light mode. AA contrast or better everywhere.
6. **One accent colour (survey orange)**, used only for: CTAs, prices/cart, the survey/comb line, human checkpoints, focus rings, form state.
7. Visuals never block content or the form. Everything readable with JS off (except the interactive demo, which has a static fallback).
8. `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a calm still version. Never use `window.addEventListener('scroll')`.
9. Develop on branch `claude/magical-ramanujan-904aka`. Push only there.

## 2. Concept: "Cultivated Ground"

A business's operations are a field. Each stalk is a unit of repetitive work. At first the field is overgrown and out of sync. Edencorp surveys the ground, combs the work into exact rows, divides it into priced plots, grows it, then transplants it into the client's own ground or keeps tending it.

**Signature moment: "the comb".** On first scroll a thin survey-orange line sweeps across the field; behind it every stalk springs (slight overshoot) from its wild position into exact rows that sway in phase. After that, the cursor sends synchronised ripples down the rows instead of scattering them.

**Motion language.** Two registers. Before cultivation: noisy, organic, out of phase. After: phase-locked, rhythmic, precise. UI motion: firm, settled, engineered. Ease-out-expo style curves (`cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`), springs with low bounce. No floaty drift, no infinite decorative loops on UI.

Borrowed from rejected concepts: the cursor becomes a **survey lens** in the audit stage (reveals contour lines beneath the field); handover is a **transplant** (a plot lifts into a fenced plot labelled "Your infrastructure").

### Scroll -> scene
- Each story section registers with `useStage(stageIndex)` which uses Motion `useScroll({target})` and writes a continuous `story` value (stage + fraction) into `sceneStore` (plain object). Scene reads it in `useFrame` and damps uniforms toward targets. No React re-renders per frame.
- Pointer: one `pointermove` listener on window writes NDC to the store.

### Scene stages (uniform targets)
| Stage | Section | comb | survey | plots | grow | lift | wilt | camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 wild | hero | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | low, close, 3/4 view |
| 1 comb | comb | 0->1 scrubbed | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | low, slightly higher |
| 2 wilt | statement | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0->0.6 | dim |
| 3 plots | capabilities | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | high overview |
| 4 process | process (sub-steps qualify/survey/choose/build/own) | 1 | survey step=1 | choose=1 | build=1.35 | own=1 | 0 | per sub-step |
| 5 calm | storefront..fit-check | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | wide calm dawn |

`humanMode` (Autonomous vs Alongside toggle) tints every 7th row's stalks survey-orange (human checkpoints).
`capHighlight` (0 none, 1-4) brightens one of four plots when hovering capabilities.

### Field shader
- Instanced tapered blade (5 segments). Per-instance attributes: wild xz, row xz, rand vec4 (height, phase, lean, tone), plot id.
- Shared terrain height fn `h(x,z)` (cheap sum of sines) used by blades and ground.
- Position = mix(wild, row, combMask) where combMask uses front = mix(-L, L, comb) with back-out easing (overshoot).
- Sway: wild = per-blade random phase/direction, larger amplitude; ordered = travelling wave along rows, uniform direction.
- Pointer: wild = bend away radially (parting). Ordered = ring ripple travelling outward.
- Colour: ink root -> bone tip, distance fog into ink. Comb front glows survey orange for a short band.
- Ground: ink plane; furrow lines when combed; contour lines when survey (stronger inside pointer lens radius); plot boundaries; fenced "your infrastructure" plot outline when own.
- Quality tiers (`quality.ts`): high 48k blades DPR<=1.5; medium 24k DPR<=1.25; low (mobile / low cores) 10k DPR 1. No WebGL -> poster only.

### Fallbacks
- First paint: `public/scene/poster-wild.webp` (rendered from the real scene via Playwright) as fixed backdrop. Canvas fades in over it once first frame renders.
- Reduced motion: no canvas, no lenis; fixed still `poster-cultivated.webp`; section reveals become opacity-only.
- Save-Data / very low tier: poster only.

## 6. Copy (final; edit here first)

### Hero (stage 0)
- H1: **Built for your ground. Nobody else's.**
- Sub: Edencorp studies how your business actually runs, then engineers the AI and software that make it faster and more profitable.
- CTAs: Check fit / See how it works

### Comb (stage 1, pinned ~250vh)
- Before: **Most of the week is the same task, done again.**
- After: **We take it over. Row by row.**
- Body: AI workforces that run customer support, data entry, outbound sales, back-office operations and QA generation end to end.
- Row annotations: Support tickets / Data entry / Outbound follow-ups / Invoice matching / QA test cases / Order updates
- Toggle: Autonomous | Alongside your team
  - Autonomous: The work runs without anyone touching it. Your people move to the work only people can do.
  - Alongside: Your team stays. Every task arrives drafted, checked and ready, with a person signing off where it matters.

### Statement (stage 2)
- **Cheap systems are the most expensive thing you can buy.**
- You pay once for the build, then again for everything it breaks. We charge for work that holds.
- Our incentive is openly selfish: we only do well when you earn more and run faster. So every feature we propose has to earn its place.

### Capabilities `#work` (stage 3)
- H2: **Four kinds of work. One standard.**
- AI workforces: Repetitive operations, handled end to end. Support, data entry, outbound, back office, QA. Fully autonomous or human-in-the-loop.
- Bespoke copilots: Built around one team's tools, data and habits, so the people you already have move faster.
- Predictive and strategic intelligence: Forecasts and decision support built from your operational and market data. Fewer guesses at the top.
- Custom software and autonomous pipelines: Platforms and pipelines built from nothing. Bring the vision and the budget, and we will build it.
- Note: Buy one piece or all four. Scope sets the price, never a package.

### Process `#process` (stage 4, pinned, 5 sub-steps)
- H2: **How an engagement actually runs.**
- Qualify / A short fit check: A call with whoever owns the decision. We confirm three things: a real bottleneck or a clear vision, a budget that fits the scope, and readiness to move on a timeline.
- Survey / We study the ground before we build on it: Under NDA we interview your staff, study your industry and competitors, map where the work gets stuck, and think hard about what would most move speed and revenue. The audit carries a deposit, credited in full to whatever you build. Serious buyers pay nothing extra.
- Choose / A private storefront, not a proposal: Every feature from the audit is listed with its scope, estimate and price. Add what you want, remove what you don't, check out. Checkout sends the build request to us.
- Build / Watch it grow: You get a live portal to follow and test the work, updates where you already talk (Slack, WhatsApp, Discord or email), a set number of revision rounds before sign-off, and direct contact throughout. Every feature carries its own estimate, because timelines depend on scope.
- Own / Take it with you, or let us tend it: Full handover means deployment to your infrastructure, the complete source code, and no further obligation. A managed retainer means we keep running, maintaining and improving it.

### Storefront demo `#storefront` (stage 5)

- Sub: After the audit you get a private storefront like this one. Try it.
- Frame label: Example storefront. Fictional company, illustrative prices.
- Fictional client: **Marlowe Freight Co.** (fictional mid-size freight brokerage)

  1. MF-01 Inbound quote desk agent: Reads quote requests, prices lanes from your rate history, drafts replies for approval. 3-4 weeks, 14,800
  2. MF-02 Carrier onboarding pipeline: Collects documents, checks insurance and authority, flags gaps before dispatch. 2-3 weeks, 9,600
  3. MF-03 Shipment status agent: Answers "where's my load" on WhatsApp and email around the clock, straight from your TMS. 2 weeks, 7,400
  4. MF-04 Invoice reconciliation: Matches carrier invoices to rate confirmations and PODs, then surfaces disputes. 3 weeks, 11,200
  5. MF-05 Dispatcher copilot: A side panel in your TMS that suggests carriers from lane history and drafts tenders. 4 weeks, 16,500
  6. MF-06 Lane margin forecast: Weekly margin forecasts by lane, with alerts when a lane starts to slip. 4-5 weeks, 18,900
  7. MF-07 Shipper prospecting engine: Researches shippers, writes first-touch outreach, books meetings for your reps. 3 weeks, 12,300
  8. MF-08 Customer portal: Branded portal for quotes, tracking and documents. 6-8 weeks, 34,000

- Checkout -> sequence: "Build request sent" + summary + "In a real storefront this goes straight to Edencorp." + Reset.

### Security `#security` (stage 5)
- H2: **Your ground. Your walls.**
- Data: NDA before we see anything / Zero data retention / An isolated environment for every client / Deploy inside your own infrastructure, with the full source code.
- Usage costs: Bring your own keys, and usage bills go straight to you from the provider. Or prepay through us: you fund a balance, and hard limits stop usage when it runs out until you top up. Never an invoice you didn't see coming. Either way, no lock-in.

### FAQ `#faq`
- H2: **Straight answers.**
- Do you build on templates? / No. Every engagement starts from an audit of your operations, and every system is built for them. We use proven models and libraries the way any engineer does, but nothing is a resold template.
- Will I see prices before I commit? / Yes. Every feature from the audit sits in your storefront with its scope, estimate and price. You pick, then check out.
- Who owns the code? / You can. Choose full handover and you get the complete source, deployed to your infrastructure, with no obligation to us. Or keep us on a retainer to run it.
- What happens to our data? / NDA from the start, zero data retention, isolated environments per client, and the option to run everything inside your own infrastructure.
- Could AI usage costs surprise us? / No. Use your own API keys, or prepay a balance through us with hard limits. We never bill usage after the fact.
- Is there a minimum budget? / No. A single workflow for a solo founder and a multi-department system are both real projects. Price follows scope.
- How long does a build take? / It depends on scope and industry, so we don't publish a standard timeline. Each feature in your storefront carries its own estimate.
- What does the audit cost? / It carries a deposit sized to the scope, credited in full toward whatever you build.
- Which industries do you work in? / All of them. The method is the same everywhere: study the operation deeply, then build exactly what it needs.
- Why pay a premium? / Because the cheap version usually gets built twice. A system that breaks costs more than it saves, and the second build costs more than doing it right once.
